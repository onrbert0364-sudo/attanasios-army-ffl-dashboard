ℹ️ How this tab works

Scores one player from 0 to 100 for a chosen decision (captain, transfer in, starting XI or bench). The decision type changes which metrics matter most. See the Weights tab.

  1. Fixture Lookup (optional): enter the gameweek, press Auto Load, then click the player's team (🏠 home or ✈️ away). This sets FDR and home/away for you. Press 🔍 Auto H2H to fill the H2H slider from the last few seasons' results at that ground.
  2. Type the name, choose position and decision type.
  3. Fill the metrics from the player's FPL profile. Hover the ⓘ next to each label to see where the number comes from.
  4. Tick any modifiers that apply (penalty taker, rotation risk, double gameweek and so on).
  5. Read the score and the breakdown bars, then + Add to Shortlist or → Send to Starting XI.

Score bands: 85+ Elite · 70–84 Strong · 55–69 Decent · 40–54 Average · under 40 Weak.

GKP/DEF: the xG slider is swapped for xGC per 90 (goals conceded expected, lower is better).

Shortlist is not saved. It clears if the page is refreshed. Send players to the XI or note them before closing.

ℹ️ How this tab works

Head-to-head between the player you'd sell (A, red) and the player you'd buy (B, green). Both are scored with the same engine as the Evaluate tab.

  1. Fill both columns with the same sources you use on the Evaluate tab. FDR here is the next gameweek only.
  2. Set Free transfers available. Choosing 0 applies the −4 hit.
  3. Press Evaluate Transfer.

How the verdict is decided: score of B minus score of A, minus 4 if it costs a hit.

  • +15 or more: ✅ Strong Transfer
  • +5 to +14: ✅ Recommended
  • 0 to +4: ⚠️ Marginal. Only do it for a reason the numbers don't capture (injury, price drop, upcoming blank).
  • Below 0: ❌ Not Recommended
  • Player B ticked as BGW: always ❌ Do Not Transfer

Caution: the −4 is an approximation. Dashboard score points are not FPL points, so treat a marginal verdict with a hit as "probably not".

Goals/xG ratio and xG overperf check matter for attackers only. Leave them at 1.0 for GKP and DEF.

ℹ️ How this tab works

Picks your best 11 from up to 15 players using FPL formation rules: 1 GKP, 3–5 DEF, 2–5 MID, 1–3 FWD.

  1. Score each squad player on the Evaluate tab with decision type Starting XI.
  2. Use → Send All to XI on the shortlist, or add players by hand here (name, position, score).
  3. Tick Doubt for anyone flagged injured or uncertain. They're shown with a warning in the result.
  4. Press Build Optimal XI. It fills the minimum per position with the highest scores, then the remaining spots with the best players left. The rest form the bench.

Save your squad: use ⬇ Export to download it as a file and ⬆ Import to reload it next week. The list is otherwise lost on refresh.

ℹ️ How this tab works

Tracks which of your eight 2026/27 chips you've used. It's saved in this browser, so it stays between visits.

  1. Set Gameweek played to the gameweek you used the chip.

The banner counts down to the Set 1 deadline and tells you how many first-half chips are still unused, because they're lost if not played by then.

2026/27 Chip Rules

  • Two of every chip (Wildcard, Free Hit, Triple Captain, Bench Boost), one set per half of the season. Eight in total.
  • Set 1 must be played before the GW19 deadline: 13:30 GMT, Saturday 2 January. Unused Set 1 chips do not carry over.
  • One chip per gameweek.
  • Free Hit can't be played in GW1. If Free Hit 1 is used in GW19, Free Hit 2 can't be used in GW20.
  • Free transfers: up to five can be banked.

Source: premierleague.com, "What's happening with FPL chips in 2026/27" and "All you need to know about changes to FPL for 2026/27" (both July 2026). Checked 19 Nov 2026.

When to Play Each Chip

🃏 Wildcard

Unlimited permanent transfers for one gameweek. Use it when three or more players need replacing, or ahead of a fixture run your squad is badly placed for. Not for a single-position fix.

⭐ Triple Captain

Captain scores ×3 instead of ×2. Best when your captain has two fixtures (double gameweek) or an easy home game. Look for a Captain score of 80+ on the Evaluate tab first.

🚀 Bench Boost

Your four bench players' points count too. Only worth it when all four have a fixture, ideally a double gameweek. Score your bench with decision type Bench to check.

🔄 Free Hit

Unlimited transfers for one gameweek only. Your squad goes back to normal afterwards. Best in a blank gameweek when many of your players have no fixture. Pick that week's XI only, ignoring long-term value.

Chip Notes

How Weights Work

Each decision type has its own set of weights. A weight is how much that metric counts towards the 0–100 score. Each set should add up to 1.00, and the total below each card warns you if it doesn't.

Example: Form weight 0.25 and a player on 6.0 form. Form is measured out of 12, so 6.0 is half marks (0.5). 0.5 × 0.25 × 100 = 12.5 points towards the score.

The thinking behind each set:

  • Captain: form and xG lead. You want ceiling, not consistency.
  • Transfer: fixture leads, because buying into bad fixtures costs more than a form difference.
  • Bench: minutes lead. A bench player only helps if he actually plays.
  • Starting XI: a balance of form, xG and fixture.

Saving: press Save Custom Weights. They're stored in this browser and reload automatically next time. Reset to Defaults wipes your custom set.

Mgr H2H is never filled automatically. Keep its weight at 0.01–0.03 or move it to H2H.

Weekly Routine

1
Wait for scores to be final. FPL finalises gameweek scores at 09:00 UK time the day after the last match, so check from then.
2
Load next week's fixtures. Evaluate tab → 🏟️ Fixture Lookup → enter the next gameweek → Auto Load.
3
Score your candidates. For each player: click his team in the fixture list → 🔍 Auto H2H → fill name, position, decision type and metrics → + Add to Shortlist.
4
Check transfers. Compare / Transfer tab: weakest player you own as A, best target as B, set free transfers → Evaluate Transfer. Check FPL's Price Change Predictor before committing.
5
Pick a captain. Score your top two or three with decision type Captain. Highest score wins, unless a flag (rotation, injury return) says otherwise.
6
Set the team. Starting XI tab → Build Optimal XI → copy it into FPL. ⬇ Export the squad so you can reload it next week.
7
Check chips. Chip Planner tab: any Set 1 chips left before the GW19 deadline?

How the Score Works

Every metric is converted to a value between 0 and 1, multiplied by its weight for the chosen decision type, then multiplied by 100. The results are added up to give the 0–100 score. If the overperformance check is above 1.5, up to 8 points are deducted.

Form0 to 12 pts/GW scale. 6.0 = 0.5.
xG per 900 to 1.0 scale. Anything above 1.0 counts as full marks (the slider goes higher, the score doesn't).
xA per 900 to 0.6 scale. Above 0.6 counts as full marks.
xGC per 90 (GKP/DEF)0 to 3.0, reversed: 0 = full marks, 3.0 = zero.
FDR2 = 1.00 · 3 = 0.75 · 4 = 0.45 · 5 = 0.25, plus 0.08 for a home game. Double gameweek ×1.8. Blank gameweek = 0.
Home/AwayHome = 1.00, Away = 0.35.
Minutes %0 to 100 scale.
H2H, Mgr H2HUsed as entered (already 0 to 1).

Score bands: 85+ Elite 🔥 · 70–84 Strong ✅ · 55–69 Decent 👍 · 40–54 Average ⚠️ · under 40 Weak ❌

Reading the breakdown bars: they're sorted by points contributed. The top bar is the "Top Factor" shown on the shortlist. A score held up almost entirely by Fixture is less reliable than one spread across form and xG.

Where to Find Each Number

Most numbers are on the player's profile on the FPL site. Click a player's name anywhere in FPL → History tab. The table has a Totals row and a Per 90 row. Scroll the table right if a column is hidden.

FormShown on the player's FPL profile. FPL's own figure: average points per match over roughly the last month.
xG per 90History tab → Per 90 row → xG column.
Watch for small samples: a player with under ~300 minutes can show huge per-90 figures from one or two good games.
xA per 90History tab → Per 90 row → xA column.
xGC per 90History tab → Per 90 row → xGC column (GKP/DEF only). This reflects the team's defence as well as the player.
FDRSet automatically when you click a team in Fixture Lookup. Otherwise use the FDR colour on the FPL fixtures page.
Minutes %Total minutes (Totals row, MP) ÷ (gameweeks played × 90) × 100. Example: 720 minutes after 10 gameweeks = 720 ÷ 900 = 80.
H2H scorePress 🔍 Auto H2H after clicking a team in Fixture Lookup. It's the selected team's win rate at that ground over recent seasons. 0.50 = no data or even.
Mgr H2HNo automatic source. Leave at 0.33 unless you know the managers' record.
Goals ÷ xG
(overperf check)		Totals row: GS ÷ xG. Use the season total xG, not the Per 90 figure. Example: 3 goals ÷ 1.7 xG = 1.76.
Leave at 1.00 for GKP and DEF, and for anyone with very few shots.

Modifiers

Double GameweekFixture value ×1.8 (capped at full marks).
Blank GameweekFixture value = 0. On the Compare tab, an incoming BGW player is always "Do Not Transfer".
Penalty takerAdds 0.25 to xG/90 before scoring.
Set piece specialistAdds 0.08 to xA/90 before scoring.
Rotation riskMinutes value ×0.75. Use when there's a midweek European game or a manager hint about rotation.
Returning from injuryMinutes value ×0.65.
Overperf checkAbove 1.5 deducts points: (ratio − 1.5) × 16, up to 8 points. Example: 1.76 → about 4 points off.

Troubleshooting

Page looks the same after an updateGitHub is serving an old copy. Add ?v= and a new number to the address, e.g. …/FPL_Decision_Dashboard.html?v=13. Check with Cmd+Option+U (view source) if unsure.
Safari shows old versionDevelop menu → Empty Caches, or open it in a Private window.
Auto Load failsWait a few seconds and press it again (the data source can be slow to wake). Otherwise use ✏️ Manual to enter the fixture yourself.
Auto H2H says "Timed out"Press it again. The first lookup for a fixture can take 10–20 seconds. After that it's remembered for 24 hours.
Auto H2H "team not found"A team code mismatch between FPL and football-data.org. Set the H2H slider by hand and let me know which team it was.
Weights reset themselvesWeights are saved per browser. Chrome and Safari keep separate copies, and a Private window saves nothing.
Shortlist or XI disappearedNeither is saved on refresh yet. Use ⬇ Export on the Starting XI tab to keep your squad.

Known Limitations

  • Metrics are typed in by hand. FPL publishes most of them, and auto-fill is the next planned update.
  • xG above 1.0 and xA above 0.6 per 90 don't score any higher.
  • Per-90 figures aren't weighted by minutes played, so small samples can inflate a score.
  • Fixture difficulty covers the next gameweek only, not a fixture run.
  • Transfers don't look at price, your bank or your current squad.
  • The −4 hit is an approximation in score points, not FPL points.
